About Guardian Security

Canadian-owned and operated since 2008

Trusted Protection Nationwide

Founded in Toronto, Guardian Security has expanded to serve clients across all Canadian provinces. Our team of over 200 licensed professionals is dedicated to keeping your property and people safe.

We hold security licenses in Ontario (License #12345), Quebec (Permit #67890), Alberta (License #54321), and British Columbia (License #98765), with full compliance in all other provinces.

Our security officers undergo rigorous training that exceeds provincial requirements, including de-escalation techniques, emergency response, and cultural sensitivity training.
